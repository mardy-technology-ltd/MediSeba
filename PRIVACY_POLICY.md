# Privacy Policy for MediSeba

**Last Updated: August 4, 2026**

**MediSeba** ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by **Mardy Technology Ltd.**, is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by MediSeba when you use our mobile application and healthcare services.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

- **Personal Identification Information:** Name, phone number, email address, profile picture.
- **Location Information:** Division, District, Upazila, and Union details provided by you to match local medical services, doctors, and blood donors.
- **Health & Appointment Information:** Appointment preferences, selected doctor specialties, and consultation history requested within the app.
- **Authentication Data:** Account login details through Firebase Authentication (including Google Sign-In credentials).

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

- To provide, operate, and maintain our healthcare and appointment booking services.
- To match users with nearby medical specialists, diagnostic services, and blood donation networks.
- To manage user accounts and provide customer support.
- To communicate updates, appointment confirmations, and service notifications.
- To ensure security and prevent fraudulent activity.

---

## 3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

- **Service Providers:** We utilize trusted third-party cloud services for app functionality, including:
  - **Firebase (Google Cloud):** Account authentication and database storage.
  - **Cloudinary:** Secure profile image storage.
- **Medical Professionals:** Relevant appointment details (e.g., patient name, contact number) are shared with selected doctors for consultation purposes.
- **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose information if required by law or to protect rights and safety.

---

## 4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. Data transmitted between the app and servers is encrypted using standard HTTPS protocol.

---

## 5. Your Rights & Data Retention

- You have the right to access, update, or correct your profile information at any time directly through the MediSeba app profile settings.
- You may request account deletion and removal of your personal data by contacting us at our support email.

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

MediSeba is not intended for use by children under 13 without parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
