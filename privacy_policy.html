Privacy Policy for MediSeba

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

MediSeba ("we", "our", or "us"), operated by Mardy Technology Ltd., is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal information is collected, used, and disclosed by MediSeba when you use our mobile application and healthcare services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, phone number, email address, profile picture.
  • Location Information: Division, District, Upazila, and Union details provided by you to match local medical services, doctors, and blood donors.
  • Health & Appointment Information: Appointment preferences, selected doctor specialties, and consultation history.
  • Authentication Data: Account login details through Firebase Authentication (including Google Sign-In credentials).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our healthcare and appointment booking services.
  • To match users with nearby medical specialists, diagnostic services, and blood donation networks.
  • To manage user accounts and provide customer support.
  • To communicate updates, appointment confirmations, and service notifications.

3. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only with trusted infrastructure providers required for app services, such as Google Firebase (Authentication & Storage) and Cloudinary (Profile Storage).

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical security measures to protect your personal data. Data transmitted between the app and servers is encrypted using standard HTTPS protocol.

5. Your Rights & Contact

You can view, update, or request deletion of your profile data directly inside the app or by contacting us.
